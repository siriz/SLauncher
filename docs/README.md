## ℹ️ About This Fork

SLauncher is a fork of LauncherX with significant enhancements:

### Major Improvements
- ✅ **Multi-language support** (English, Korean, Japanese)
- ✅ **Real-time language switching** (no restart required)
- ✅ **Tab management system** with color coding
- ✅ **Enhanced UI/UX** improvements
- ✅ **System tray integration**
- ✅ **Global hotkey support** (Ctrl+Space)
- ✅ **Backup & Restore** - export/import settings, items and tabs
